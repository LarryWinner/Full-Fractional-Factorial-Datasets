Dataset:  bean_factorial_cc.csv

Source: W.G. Cochran and G.M. Cox (1957). "Experimental Designs, 2nd Ed.",
Wiley, NY. p. 189.

Description: Results from a 2^4 factorial design on bean yield
within 2 incomplete blocks nested within 2 replicates.
Factors: D = Dung, N=Nitrochalk, P=Superphosphate, K=Muriate of potash.
All were Absent (-1) or present (1).
