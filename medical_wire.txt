Dataset: medical_wire.dat"

Source: B. Nepal, S. Mohanty, L. Kay (2013). "Quality Improvement of
Medical Wire Manufacturing Process," Quality Improvement, Vol. 25, pp. 151-163

Description: Fractional factorial experiment relating 4 factors to
Yield Strength/Ultimate Tensile Strength (%) in Medical Wire. Run
in 3 Blocks with 5 replicate measurements per wire spool.
Factors: Drawing Machine, Die Reduction Angle, Die Bearing Length,
Supply Diameter. Each factor at 2 levels.
Experiment run as 2^(4-1) in each block, with I=ABCD

Variables/Columns
Spool ID     7-8
Block 16
Factor A Level   24   /* 0=Low, 1=High  */
Factor B Level   32   /* 0=Low, 1=High  */
Factor C Level   40   /* 0=Low, 1=High  */
Factor D Level   48   /* 0=Low, 1=High  */
YS/UTS (percent)  50-56
