Dataset: mtrcyclewheel.dat

Source: K.S. Tan, S.V. Wong, R.S. Radin Umar, A.M.S. Hamouda, and N.K. Gupta (2009).
"Impact Behavior Modeling of Motorcycle Front Wheel-Tire assembly,"
International Journal of Automotive Technology, Vol. 10, #3, pp. 329-339.

Description: Results of a 2^(5-1) fractional factorial experiment measuring Y = crush radius
on front wheel of a motorcycle tire. Factors and levels are (where D is confounded with SMPG interaction):
S = Impact Speed (meters/second) (3, 6)
M = Impact Mass (kg)   (51.18, 101.33)
P = Tire Pressure (kPa)  (148, 252)
G = Striker Contact Geometry (radius= 0.03, 0.10)
D = Impact offset Distance (m) (height= 0, 0.108)
